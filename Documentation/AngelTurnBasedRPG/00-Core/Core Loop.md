## The Loop (Plain Language)

The player wakes up in a room, at first small, and fights demons. The player can sense something protecting them, but can't make it out yet. After some scripted battles, the player can make out the figure as a guardian angel. The player and guardian angel can talk, but are unsure of why the player is in this place. The game relies on skill — no equipment, just leveling up with the [[Divine Light System]] in classic turn-based combat akin to Final Fantasy. Each floor represents a Sphere, with explorable areas between battles where lore tablets can be found. Each Sphere has gate demons that block access to deeper areas of the floor, one per ascension rank within that Sphere, and a main boss that guards the entrance to the next Sphere. After each ascension the angel can choose one of three permanent upgrades — some are stat boosts or passives, some grant new magic attacks. All upgrades are kept across ascensions. In NG+ the player carries their previous upgrade choices and selects from the remaining unchosen options, allowing all 27 upgrades to be collected across three runs — and on the third, to face the secret true final boss [[Asmodai]]. Throughout the game, lore tablets found during exploration and occasional enemy dialogue piece together the story of why the player is moving through the three Spheres.

## The Three Conditions

The entire game is built around three repeating conditions per rank:

1. **Fight** — defeat enemies in the current area to fill Divine Light and grow stats passively
2. **Overcome** — defeat the gate demon that blocks access to the next area or Sphere
3. **Ascend** — spend the full Divine Light bar to rise, then choose one upgrade

None of these can be skipped or reordered. All three must happen in roughly this sequence every rank.

## Floor and Progression Structure

Each Sphere is one floor — a physical explorable space divided into areas. Gate demons block passage between areas within a floor. The Sphere boss blocks passage to the next floor entirely.

**Sphere 1 (Floor 1)**
- Area 1 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Ronove]] (Gate: Angel → Archangel)
- Area 2 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Andras]] (Gate: Archangel → Principality)
- Area 3 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Bael]] (Sphere Boss — gate to Sphere 2)

**Sphere 2 (Floor 2)**
- Area 1 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Focalor]] (Gate: Principality → Power)
- Area 2 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Vepar]] (Gate: Power → Virtue)
- Area 3 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Furfur]] (Gate: Virtue → Dominion)
- Area 4 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Paimon]] (Sphere Boss — gate to Sphere 3)

**Sphere 3 (Floor 3)**
- Area 1 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Gaap]] (Gate: Dominion → Throne)
- Area 2 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Ipos]] (Gate: Throne → Cherubim)
- Area 3 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Alloces]] (Gate: Cherubim → Seraphim)
- Area 4 → common enemies, lore tablets → [[Beleth]] (Sphere Boss — true ending / NG+ trigger)

**[[Naberius]]** roams across all three floors as a random encounter — no gate role, can appear in any area at any Sphere.

> Note: Sphere 1 has only two gate demons and two ascension ranks with gate conditions. Principality → Power is gated by Bael and the passage to Sphere 2 itself, not a named demon. This gives Bael additional narrative weight as the first true barrier between Spheres.

## What the Player Feels at Each Stage

**Early game (Sphere 1 — ranks 1–3)**
Feels somewhat easy, still in the learning phase. Enemies are strange in appearance but battles aren't too difficult. The first real difficulty spike should come from the gate demons. Magic is not yet available at Angel rank — the player fights purely physically until the first ascension.

**Mid game (Sphere 2 — ranks 4–6)**
Battles become more complex as enemies grow more chaotic and status effects become a real problem for unprepared players. Gate demons should feel like pattern fights — understanding their mechanic is the key to surviving them. Magic attacks start to come online depending on upgrade choices.

**Late game (Sphere 3 — ranks 7–9)**
Fights feel both quicker and more dangerous. The angel's power is undeniable but the demons match it. Players with strong upgrade stacks and good mana management should feel in control; players who ignored magic or spread upgrades thin will feel the pressure.

**NG+ runs**
The game should feel easier and faster to start, rubber-banding in difficulty near the mid-to-late game as a true test of an ascended angel's power. The third run — with all 27 upgrades stacked — should feel overwhelming in the best sense. Asmodai waits at the end of it as the ultimate test.

## What Is NOT in the Loop

- No XP — Divine Light is the only progression resource
- No inventory or items — upgrades only
- No separate levelling outside of ascension
- Slight exploration between battles — exists for lore and atmosphere, not mechanics

> If a new mechanic idea comes up during development, check it against this list first. If it adds a second resource or a reason to grind outside of combat, it probably doesn't belong.
